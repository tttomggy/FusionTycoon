# Give every orb a job, not a skin

The top Roblox collect-and-grind games keep players grinding by giving same-rarity units **different jobs**, not different paint. Pet Simulator 99 does it with an enchant loadout and a single Ultimate, Anime Vanguards with rolled traits, Grow a Garden with ability pets that mutate crops, and Steal a Brainrot with event-only traits. They then stack **parallel progression tracks on different clocks**: minute-scale events, hour-scale rebirths that unlock whole new systems, day-scale offline growth and quests, and month-scale mastery and collection. Harris is right that Fusion Tycoon's 17 orbs add nothing today, because three identical reskins per tier give a player no reason to want one over another. The research says the fix is to make them differ, not to cut them. Concretely, the recommendation is to reorganise the orbs into **six element families (36 orbs, all 17 current ids kept)**. Each family's orbs carry one tier-scaled ability that works while the orb is on a pedestal and **doubles during that family's lab weather**. Same-family fusions keep the family, which turns "fuse up a Star line" into the core long-term chase. Exclusive orbs should come mainly from **earned, free sources** (events, Index rows, quest milestones, a deterministic Recycler). Those are exempt from Roblox's odds-disclosure rules and immune to the "pay-to-win" backlash that hit Steal a Brainrot. Anything sold should be guaranteed (never random), unstealable and unfusable. Themed collections should copy the *format* of Italian brainrot and anime, never the names. Tung Tung Tung Sahur is the subject of an active US federal trademark fight, and anime games with real franchise assets have been taken down. Everything below is staged so a solo developer can ship it in roughly five steps of one to two weeks each.

## Same-rarity units differ by role, and visible procs beat flat stats

Every successful collect game separates **how strong a unit is** (rarity, variant, size) from **what it does** (the build choice). Pet Simulator 99 moved enchants off individual pets and onto a player loadout: **6 free slots unlocked by Rank 20, plus 3 bought for 400, 800 and 1,200 R$**. Same-type books stack at **100%, 60%, 38.3% and 27.5% effectiveness**, while proc and companion books (Magic Orb, Midas Touch, Magnet) do not diminish ([PS Wiki: Enchants](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Enchants_(Pet_Simulator_99))). Those diminishing returns are a cheap, tunable way to force players to diversify instead of stacking one stat. On top of the loadout, PS99 lets a player equip **only one Ultimate**. Ultimates unlock at Rebirth 4, charge from pet damage and taps, and level up with use ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/pet-simulator-99/ultimates)). Area-wide **Flags** last 5 minutes and buff everyone nearby ([Bloxodes: Flags](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/pet-simulator-99/flags)). The older Pet Simulator X took a different route and put 1 to 4 enchants *on each pet*. That design produced the copy-stat pattern: **Companion** made a pet worth 50 to 75% of your best four pets' average, and **Best Friend** made it worth 150%. The pattern kept a favourite low-tier pet useful as the player progressed ([PS Wiki: Enchants (PSX)](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Enchants_(Pet_Simulator_X))).

Players do not value all abilities equally, and the community rankings agree on the main point. PS99 tier lists put **behavioural, on-screen procs** at the top: Chest Mimic, Lucky Block and Chest Breaker are S-tier. Narrow luck books such as Huge Hunter sit in C-tier, as "niche... won't help you reach more areas." The two lists do disagree on individual books, which shows the meta depends on the player's goal ([Ginx](https://www.ginx.tv/en/?p=59566); [DroidGamers](https://www.droidgamers.com/guides/pet-simulator-99-enchantments-tier-list/)). Anime Vanguards shows the most memorable kind of rare roll, the **trade-off**. Its 0.1% Monarch trait gives **+300% damage, but the unit is "limited to One Placement only"**. Plain stat traits (Range, Vigor and Swift, each rolled 26% of the time) are filler that tier lists say "should be replaced" ([Deltia's](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=349658); [Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=595841)). Role also matters. The Fortune trait gives farm units +20% gold and other units −10% upgrade cost, so the same trait suits some units and not others.

Grow a Garden turns pets into **ability carriers** that act on the world on a timer. Disco Bee applies a 125x Disco mutation about 12% of the time every 19 minutes. Dragonfly turns one random fruit Gold every 5 minutes. Butterfly strips every mutation from a fruit that has more than five and applies Rainbow, a *conversion* that makes the player decide when to let it fire ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/all-pets-give-mutation-grow-garden?key4=flipboardroblox)). Buffs **stack across copies**: one Golden Lab gives a 10% dig chance and three give about 30%, with up to 8 pets equipped ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/do-pet-buffs-stack-grow-garden)). That stacking is what drives demand for duplicates. Grow a Garden's value formula multiplies a variant (Gold or Rainbow) by *one plus the sum* of environmental mutations ([AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/stacking-mutations-in-grow-a-garden-2-how-the-multiplier-math-works/)). The exact multipliers conflict between sources, with Gold at 15x or 20x and Rainbow at 40x or 50x. Fusion Tycoon's additive stacking (Rainbow + Celestial = ×31) already follows that safe shape. Steal a Brainrot layers **event-only traits** on its mutations: Rain 2.5x, Glitch 5x, Nyan 6x. Two sources with worked examples show the multipliers *adding* ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/steal-a-brainrot-mutations-and-traits-list/amp/); [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/steal-brainrot-all-traits-multipliers)). The result is that otherwise identical units differ by *history*, and that history drives trade value.

Two more patterns round out the catalogue. Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity makes pets within a rarity differ in **which stat** they boost (bubbles, coins or gems). It also gives every duplicate value by letting players **craft a Shiny from 16 Commons, 12 Rares or Epics, 10 Legendaries or 8 Secrets** ([Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/bubble-gum-simulator-infinity-pets-guide)). Sol's RNG has no pets at all, but it creates timing decisions through **conditions**. Biome-exclusive auras such as Starfall's Comet (1 in 12,000) only drop while that biome is active, so players time potions to biomes ([Of Zen and Computing](https://ofzenandcomputing.com/sols-rng-all-biomes-guide/)). Fisch's rod enchants do the same with weather: Storming gives +95% luck and 50% Electric fish, but only in rain ([Stealthy Gaming](https://stealthygaming.com/all-fisch-enchantments-effects/)).

The failure modes are just as consistent across games. Anime Last Stand inflated its reroll currency until "some players now hold hundreds of thousands of rerolls" and "standard rerolls feel meaningless." Its worst backlash came from a Robux bundle to "instantly evolve Goku," which put power progression "locked behind Robux" ([Roonby](https://roonby.com/2026/01/15/anime-last-stand-is-dead-again-2026/)). Three lessons follow for Fusion Tycoon. Abilities should be **deterministic per orb**, so the game does not need a reroll economy that inflates. They should be **visible**, firing on screen rather than adding an invisible "+5%". And they should never be something Robux shortcuts.

## Retention runs on parallel tracks with different clocks

PS99 runs at least four progression tracks at once, each on its own time scale:

| Track | How it works | Typical time scale |
|---|---|---|
| Zones | Unlocked with coins | Minutes to hours |
| Rebirths | Shrines about every 25 areas; reset coins and area progress, keep pets | One reset per stretch of zones |
| Ranks | Quests earn stars; stars buy rewards; max rank 35; star cost rises from 13 at Rank 1 to 484 at Rank 14 | Days to weeks |
| Mastery | Per-activity levels up to 99 | Months |

Rank rewards are mostly *capacity*, meaning equip slots and egg slots ([PS Wiki: Ranks](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ranks_(Pet_Simulator_99))). The most transferable idea is that **rebirths unlock systems, not just numbers**. Each PS99 rebirth gives a flat +75% pet strength, but the headline rewards are new features: Clans and Auto Hatch at R2, Pet Teams, the Enchant Loadout and Index Rewards at R3, Ultimates, a second world and Mastery at R4 ([HoldToReset](https://holdtoreset.com/pet-simulator-99-rebirth-system-explained/)). Mastery adds a re-engagement hook. Maxing every mastery earns a cape, and **if a new mastery is added you must max it too to keep the cape** ([DungeonPath](https://dungeonpath.com/posts/pet-simulator-99/mastery-quests-guide/)).

Steal a Brainrot uses rebirths differently. Each rebirth costs cash **plus specific named brainrots**. Rebirth 1, for example, needs Trippi Troppi and Gangster Footera, and each rebirth adds a slot and new steal or defence gear ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/steal-a-brainrot-rebirth-guide-levels-and-rewards/)). That turns "collect" into "collect *this*," and it keeps mid-rarity units useful after better ones arrive. The 2025 mega-hits also added return-visit triggers that classic tycoons lacked. Grow a Garden's co-owner credited "the whole idea of the garden growing offline" for its rise to a peak of **22.3M concurrent users** ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)). Its shops restock **every five minutes** with limited stock ([Deltia's](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=207402)). Sol's RNG has sustained **2.2B visits** on rolls, timed biomes, crafting and a collection that "serves as the scoreboard" ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/sols-rng)).

Events are acquisition, not retention. Grow a Garden's Summer update hit **21.3M CCU and fell to 7.6M within an hour** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)). Steal a Brainrot sits about **99% below its 25.8M peak**, and "each event peak was followed by a steeper drop than the previous one" ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)). Grow a Garden's developer floated dropping weekly events because they were "a bit too hard to keep fresh and new" and were crowding out bug fixes ([ShaneTheGamer](https://www.shanethegamer.com/esports-news/no-more-weekly-updates-grow-a-garden-dev-teases-a-new-era/)). Roblox's own guidance suits a solo developer better. It ties D7 retention to "short- and long-term goals" and D30 to **small updates every 2–4 weeks and larger ones every 2–3 months**, though it publishes no numeric benchmarks ([Roblox Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/analytics/retention.md)). What holds the floor between spikes is the set of long tracks: ranks, mastery and the index. Fusion Tycoon's long tracks are currently thin. It has rebirths (with two feature unlocks), the Index and a quest chain, but nothing that gives a player a *reason to want a specific orb*.

## Exclusives carry prestige when earned and legal risk when sold randomly

BIG Games uses three channels for top-tier pets, and a single PS99 update rotates all three:

- **Guaranteed Robux packs.** The Farm Pack contained two Huges, a Titanic and a Gargantuan.
- **Robux-boosted chance.** Examples are the Exclusive Farm Egg and the **3,250 R$ Huge Hunter pass**, whose wiki page warns that a Huge is "not guaranteed" ([PS Wiki: Gamepasses](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Gamepasses_(Pet_Simulator_99))).
- **Free earned routes.** These include a weight contest, Time Trials chests (daily-capped, with **+20% streak and +50% maxed-runs bonuses**), crafting recipes, and an hourly "Biggest Hatch" contest for a Titanic ([BIG Games Update 61](https://www.biggames.io/post/pet-simulator-99-update-61)).

The Huge-A-Tron converts exclusive pets into a guaranteed Huge egg, but it asks for a Robux top-up below 100 points ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/how-to-get-a-huge-pet-in-pet-simulator-99/)). The newer hits lean on **attendance**. Steal a Brainrot runs seven admin-only events whose traits multiply income 3x to 6x, plus hourly weather ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/all-steal-a-brainrot-events-weather-admin-and-spawnable-events-guide/)). Grow a Garden held a live Glass Animals concert with "exclusive rewards" for those who attended ([Deltia's](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=382559)). In both, the exclusivity is "you had to be there," it is free, and it costs the developer almost nothing. Fusion Tycoon already has the same machinery: lab weather every 15 minutes, event-only mutations and admin-strength events.

Roblox's **Paid Random Items** policy decides which of these channels Fusion Tycoon can safely copy. It covers anything random bought "directly or indirectly with Robux," including through currency bought with Robux. It names egg hatches, "combination items" ("synthesizing rare eggs for a chance at better results") and **probability modifiers such as luck boosts, pity systems and enhanced drops** ([Roblox Creator Hub](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)). The policy sets four requirements:

- Every outcome must be shown with numeric odds before purchase.
- A bare (i) icon is "not sufficient."
- A modifier's numerical effect must be explained up front and **updated live while it is active**.
- `PolicyService` flags (`ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted`, `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed`) must be honoured.

Randomised rewards "earned through actions that cost no Robux or in-game currency" are **exempt**. Because Fusion Tycoon sells cash packs, its gacha and fusion already count as paid random items. The repo handles this: `FusionConfig.FormatOdds` builds every odds display from the same functions the rolls use, and shop products carry `PolicyRestricted`. The consequence for orb abilities is direct. Any ability that changes gacha luck, mutation chance or fusion success becomes a probability modifier on a paid random item. It must therefore feed the same luck and bonus inputs that `FormatOdds` reads, so the odds board updates whenever such an orb is displayed or wakes up.

The backlash record shows where sold scarcity goes wrong. The PSX NFTs drew "disappointment and… disgust" and charges that the game was "already pay-to-win" ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/pet-simulator-x-faces-backlash-from-fans-after-nft-release/)). A **$349.99** Titanic plush with a code-locked pet was cut by $100 within about two days ([Mobile Matters](https://mobilematters.gg/roblox/pet-simulator-x-plush-price-drop-after-community-backlash)). Steal a Brainrot was criticised for pay-to-win items and sold admin powers, and for "viral videos of children crying over stolen Brainrots" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)). The legal climate is tightening too. A B.C. class action alleges that mystery boxes in Roblox games are "functionally equivalent to gambling" ([Canadian Gaming Business](https://www.canadiangamingbusiness.com/2025/09/26/bc-lawsuit-alleges-roblox-gambling/)). A University of Sydney study found manipulative patterns, including spending prompts after losses, in **14 of 15** popular Roblox games ([University of Sydney](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/05/17/inside-the-pay-to-play-world-of-roblox-targeting-children-.html)). Steal a Brainrot also brought removed events (Bloodmoon, Candy) back during admin abuse. That is harmless for free rewards, but it would be a trust problem if those exclusives had been *sold* as "never again." For a kids' tycoon the safe split follows from all of this. **Top-tier exclusives should be earned. Anything sold should be guaranteed and cosmetic or convenience-shaped. No paid item should ever be stealable.**

## Brainrot sells the format, not the names, and Tung Tung Sahur is in federal court

The famous Italian brainrot characters are AI-generated TikTok memes from early 2025. Tung Tung Tung Sahur was made by an Indonesian creator, Noxa, in about 15 minutes with seven prompts ([WUNC/NPR](https://www.wunc.org/2026-08-21/a-battle-over-italian-brainrot-could-shape-who-owns-ai-art)). A French agency, **Mementum Lab**, now represents him and others. It says it has licensed Tung Tung Sahur to Fortnite, Plants vs. Brainrots and toy makers, and it claims 22 more characters ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/tung-tung-sahur-is-at-the-center-of-a-bizarre-federal-custody-battle-over-brainrot-characters-3381733/)). The dispute with Steal a Brainrot went through four stages:

1. **September 2025.** Mementum sent Steal a Brainrot a cease-and-desist, and the game *pulled the character* for about two and a half months ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)).
2. **November 26, 2025.** The game's owners sued for a declaration that Mementum has no enforceable rights.
3. **April 2026.** Mementum counter-claimed for trademark infringement, seeking a share of profits it puts in the tens of millions.
4. **Now.** The motions hearing is set for **December 9, 2026**, with no ruling on the merits ([Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/news/intellectual-property-news/tung-tung-sahur-trademark); [Haynes Boone](https://www.haynesboone.com/news/publications/bell-and-mason-in-texas-lawyer-ai-slop-or-ip-gold-the-business-of-brainrot)).

A separate company has a pending US toy trademark on the name, published September 15, 2026 ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/991/71/tung-tung-tung-99171194.html)). Whether AI-made characters can be copyrighted at all is unsettled, but that does not help a solo developer. Even the biggest brainrot game, with a legal team behind it, complied first and argued later.

Anime carries a longer track record of takedowns. In December 2023, Anime Adventures (1.9B+ visits) and both Anime Fighting Simulators went private, reportedly after DMCA notices over My Hero Academia assets filed by the studio behind the licensed MHA Roblox game ([Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/anime-adventures/private)). Roblox removes content on a valid notice and terminates repeat infringers. Its guidance says infringement happens even when the content is unmonetised, credited, or something "others use too" ([Roblox DMCA Guidelines](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/dmca-guidelines)). Rights holders can now file up to **250 takedown links per request** through Rights Manager ([Roblox Rights Manager](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/rights-manager)). The documented pattern is that enforcement follows when the rights holder has a licensed game on the platform. Both anime and brainrot now have one.

Timing argues against chasing the meme as well. Search interest in Italian brainrot peaked in May 2025, and later brainrot games peaked far lower: Escape Tsunami For Brainrots hit about 5M before falling to about 164k, and Swing Obby for Brainrots reached only about 87k ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)). Copyright does not protect ideas or styles ([Roblox DMCA Guidelines](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/dmca-guidelines)). The claims being litigated target **names and images**. The lowest-risk path is therefore original absurd hybrids in the same format, with new names, new designs and original sounds. The original meme audio is also a content problem. The Tralalero Tralala track contains blasphemy, and some Bombardiro Crocodilo narration describes bombing children ([Wikipedia: Italian brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_brainrot)). Roblox's "Mild" maturity tier allows burp-and-fart humour and still reaches the 5–8 Kids tier ([Roblox Content Maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity)). A goofy, slightly gross tone fits the audience, provided it uses no borrowed lines.

## The Fusion Tycoon design: six orb families with jobs

### Six element families replace three reskins per tier

The 17 current orbs regroup into **six element families**, each with one orb per tier: 36 orbs, 19 of them new. Each family is tied to one of the six lab events that already exist in `EventConfig`. Saves store the item **Id**, so every existing id is kept and only display names may change. The table below shows current orbs in **bold** and proposed new orbs in plain text.

| Family (wakes during) | Common | Rare | Epic | Legendary | Mythic | Secret |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⚡ Volt (Power Surge) | **Spark Cell** | **Volt Coil** | **Plasma Orb** | Storm Engine | Tesla Heart | Overload |
| ☀ Solar (Golden Rain) | **Copper Bolt** (rename "Sun Bolt") | **Ember Core** | **Solar Flare** | **Nova Heart** | Phoenix Core | Dawn Crown |
| ❄ Frost (Night) | Snow Bead | **Frost Crystal** | Moon Shard | Glacier Heart | Aurora Stone | Absolute Zero |
| 🌈 Prism (Rainbow Storm) | **Glass Shard** | Prism Drop | Spectrum Lens | **Quantum Crown** | Rainbow Engine | **Eternity Prism** |
| 🌑 Void (Void Moon) | Dark Bead | Shadow Coil | **Void Prism** | **Rift Engine** | **Singularity** | **Event Horizon** |
| ⭐ Star (Meteor Shower) | Star Dust | Comet Chip | Meteor Core | **Star Core** | **Genesis Stone** | Supernova |

The art budget stays manageable if one parametric orb builder takes a family motif (core shape, colour, particle type) and a tier recipe (size, rings, satellite count). A solo developer then authors six motifs, not 36 models. Pedestal income stays per tier, as in `TycoonConfig.PedestalCashPerSecond` (Common $3/s up to Secret $50,000/s). What changes between same-tier orbs is the ability.

### Each family does one job, scaled by tier

An orb's ability is active **only while it sits on a pedestal**, which makes pedestals the equivalent of PS99's equip slots. Same-family abilities stack at PS99's **100 / 60 / 38 / 27.5%** for the first through fourth copies, so a lab of ten Star orbs is weaker than a mixed lab. During a family's own event the ability works at **×2**. A mutated orb gets ability strength × (1 + 0.1 × (stacked multiplier − 1)), capped at ×2, so a Golden orb is ×1.1 and a Rainbow or Celestial orb hits the ×2 cap. The numbers below are starting points to feed into `tools/econ_sim.py`, not final tuning.

| Family | Ability (on a pedestal) | C / R / E / L / M / S | Type and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volt | **Overclock**: generator output + | 5 / 8 / 12 / 18 / 25 / 35% | Income; the only one auto-display can score in $/s |
| Solar | **Midas Touch**: every 10 min, chance to turn one plain orb of its tier or lower (in your inventory) Golden | 15 / 25 / 35 / 50 / 70 / 100% | Visible conversion proc (GAG Dragonfly, PS99 Midas); feeds same-mutation fusion |
| Frost | **Deep Freeze**: offline earnings rate + (base 25%) | +2 / 3 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 pts, total capped at 50% | Come-back-tomorrow hook (GAG offline growth) |
| Prism | **Refract**: mutation chance on pulls and fusions × | 1.05 / 1.08 / 1.12 / 1.18 / 1.25 / 1.35 | Probability modifier; must show on the odds board |
| Void | **Stabilize**: fusion success + (fusions of its tier or lower, never Mythic→Secret) | +1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 7 pts, ability total capped at +10 | Probability modifier; the Secret chase stays intact |
| Star | **Stargazer**: gacha luck (Legendary/Mythic/Secret rates, like rebirth luck) × | 1.03 / 1.05 / 1.08 / 1.12 / 1.17 / 1.25 | Probability modifier through the existing luck path |

A worked example gives a feel for the scale. Four Secret Stars give luck × (1 + 0.25 × 2.26) ≈ **×1.56**, or about **×2.1 during a Meteor Shower**. On top of Rebirth 3's ×1.15 luck, that roughly 2.5-fold lift takes the Secret pull rate from 0.002% to about 0.005%. The orb is worth chasing without breaking the curve. Engineering-wise, put all of this in **one pure `AbilityConfig` module**, read by both server and client, following the `MutationConfig` and `EventConfig` pattern:

- Volt's output enters through `GetIncomeInputs`, so `GetPassiveCashPerSecond` stays the one income formula.
- Star's luck enters wherever `RebirthConfig.GetLuck` does today.
- Prism and Void enter `FormatOdds`' multiplier and bonus parameters.

That routing satisfies Roblox's requirement that a modifier's effect be disclosed and updated live, because the odds board then always shows what is rolled.

### Fusion, pedestals, heists and events each get a hook

**Fusion keeps the family.** If every input shares a family, a success stays in that family. If the inputs are mixed, the result's family is drawn at random from the inputs' families. This mirrors the existing "a success keeps the lowest shared mutation" rule, and the Fuse panel can predict it the same way ("✨ Stays STAR"). It is the main grind lever. To build a Secret Star, a player has to grow a Star line through every tier, so even Common Star Dust has a purpose. Fuse All can keep its current behaviour (unmutated pairs, Common to Epic) and should prefer same-family pairs.

**Pedestals stay automatic.** Auto-display keeps ranking by `GetStackCashPerSecond` with Volt's contribution included. Ties break toward family diversity. This preserves Playtest 7's "players never choose" decision. The single deliberate choice comes later, in the Core slot (Stage 4). Pedestal chips and the reveal card gain one line: the family glyph and the ability ("⭐ Luck ×1.08"). Waking pedestals show it with orb particles or the orb shell, **not brighter lights**, because CLAUDE.md caps pedestal lights at 0.8–1.6 brightness.

**Heists raise the stakes without selling power.** A stolen orb's ability pauses while it is carried (`BeingStolen`) and transfers on delivery. Stealing someone's Secret Star therefore takes their luck, which creates exactly the stakes that made Steal a Brainrot clip-worthy. One hard rule protects the never list: **no ability on an orb that can come out of the gacha may touch heist or combat**. Cash packs make the gacha a Robux-reachable source, so a gacha orb with a defensive ability would be lab protection sold indirectly. Heist-utility abilities live only on earned exclusives (below). Those abilities give information or recovery, never protection. An example is **Watchtower**: when a thief grabs from your lab, you get a ping and a beam to the carrier. Because CLAUDE.md caps `Highlight`s, Watchtower must not use one.

**Events become personal.** Each 15-minute weather wakes one family (×2), so every slot on the schedule means "my Volt orbs are surging" for someone. That turns the existing `EventConfig` clock into the timing layer that Sol's RNG gets from biomes and Fisch from weather enchants. Events also become the main free source of exclusives.

### Exclusive orbs come from earned sources first and guaranteed sales second

All exclusives share three rules. They are **not fusable**: they are end items, which also keeps paid ones out of the "combination item" clause. They show on a separate Index page. And only *earned* exclusives count toward the Index income bonus, so no one can pay to complete the Index.

| Source | Example | Cost to player | Policy status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Event drops | Meteor Shower cores: 1% chance of **Comet Shard** (Star exclusive, Stargazer + Watchtower); Night: **Lantern Moth** | Free (be there) | Exempt; show the 1% anyway |
| Index family row | Complete a family's 6 tiers in any variant → **Crowned [Family]** orb, ability ×1.5 | Free | Exempt |
| Quest chain milestones | #25, #50, #100 each award a named exclusive | Free | Exempt |
| Lab Trials (daily, capped, streak) | PS99 Time Trials shape: 3 runs/day, +20% streak points → points shop with one rotating exclusive | Free | Exempt |
| **Lab Recycler** | Feed spare orbs for Fusion Energy (C 1, R 4, E 15, L 60, M 400; ×2 if mutated). 2,000 → a **Giant** Legendary of the family you pick; 10,000 → a Giant Mythic. Giant = 1.5× model scale, ×3 income, ability ×1.5 | Spare orbs | Deterministic, so not random. The Huge-A-Tron without the Robux top-up |
| Scheduled "Fusion Malfunction" (monthly admin-abuse style) | Fusions have a small chance to spit out a **Lab Accident** orb (themed set, below) | Fusion inputs | Part of fusion odds, so the board must list the % while it runs |
| Shop (Robux) | **Founder's Orb**: one specific orb, guaranteed, power capped at an earned Legendary's level, non-PvP ability; plus cosmetic **orb skin packs** | Robux, live price | Not random. **Unstealable**, unfusable, no "never again" copy, no timer, never offered after a loss or theft |

The Recycler is the most valuable single addition. It is the duplicate sink that BGSI's Shiny crafting and PS99's Golden/Rainbow machines provide. It gives every spare pull lasting value, and it creates a fully deterministic, kid-legible path to the game's most prestigious orbs. Do **not** ship mystery orb packs. If one is ever wanted, it needs full odds behind a labelled "Odds" button, `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` handling, and a direct-buy alternative priced at expected value, as the policy describes ([Roblox Creator Hub](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)).

### Themed sets copy the format and own the names

**Lab Accidents** is the brainrot answer. These are absurd hybrid creatures sealed in orbs, which fits a game literally about fusing things. Each gets a sing-song name and an original chant sound, and carries a **trade-off ability**, the Monarch pattern players remember. Three examples:

- **Toastopus** earns ×3 on its own pedestal, but your generators run −10%.
- **Disco Cactus** boosts every ability by +50% during any event and earns nothing outside events.
- **Gloopy the Lava Lamp** copies the ability of the best other displayed orb at 50%, a copy-stat in the Best Friend style.

Auto-display handles a trade-off orb by computing plot income with and without it and keeping the better arrangement. Before shipping any name, search the Roblox catalogue and the brainrot fan wikis. Avoid every Mementum-claimed name and the "Sahur / Tung / Tralala / -ini" signatures, and keep humour within Roblox's "Mild" tier.

**Anime** fits best as an **Awakened** look for Mythic and Secret orbs, earned through Family Mastery: energy-flare auras, speed lines and a power-up sting. Use no character likenesses, technique names, voice clips or franchise assets.

Because orbs are data-driven (one `ItemConfig` entry each), a rename or reskin after a claim costs one config edit. That is the cheap insurance that Steal a Brainrot's two-and-a-half-month character removal argues for.

### A long-term purpose on five clocks

| Clock | Loop | What drives it |
|---|---|---|
| Seconds | Pull, fuse, watch a Midas proc | Existing gacha and fusion |
| 15 minutes | Your family wakes (×2), event exclusives drop | Existing event clock |
| An hour | Rebirth, with a **feature** per rebirth | R3: Core slot + signatures; R4: Recycler/Giants; R5: Family Mastery |
| A day | Frost offline earnings, Lab Trials streak, daily quests | Existing offline and quest systems |
| Weeks to months | Family Mastery, Crowned orbs, **the Six Secrets** | New capstone |

Rebirth unlocks should follow PS99's "reset for a feature" model: keep the existing ×(1 + 0.5n) income, and give each rebirth a headline system. From Rebirth 3, each rebirth should also require **owning (not consuming)** a listed set, for example "an Epic of three different families." This is Steal a Brainrot's "collect *this*" without the feel-bad of sacrificing orbs.

**Family Mastery** works per family, levels 1–20. XP comes from pulling, fusing and displaying that family's orbs. Every 5 levels the family's ability gets +5%, and all six maxed earns a lab-coat title that gains a star for each family added in later updates. That gives every update a re-engagement reason without PS99's take-it-away rule.

The **capstone** is the clear purpose Harris asked for: **own a Secret from all six families**. That unlocks the **Grand Collider**, a lab centrepiece whose one-time use fuses the six into a unique, unstealable trophy orb, and puts the player's name on a server board. With the first Secret at about 8.5 hours median in the current sim (with huge spread) and family carry required, this is a months-long goal. Every 2–4-week update can then add one exclusive or one Lab Accident set, and every 2–3-month update one new family. That matches Roblox's own cadence guidance rather than the weekly treadmill Grow a Garden itself found unsustainable.

Hold back two things. The first is random rerollable traits, which bring a reroll economy that inflates, as Anime Last Stand's did. The second is any thief-side ability, because the existing weapons already cover offence and the crying-kid clips are the genre's worst press.

## Conclusion

The research reframes Harris's complaint. Seventeen orbs is not too many; seventeen orbs with nothing to *do* is too few jobs. The games that hold players for months give a mid-tier unit a role, a timing window and a path to something bigger. Fusion Tycoon already has unusually good raw material for that. Its fusion rule ("keeps the lowest shared mutation") extends naturally into "keeps the shared family." Its 15-minute weather clock is a ready-made schedule of six events for six families. And its single odds formatter is exactly the plumbing Roblox requires once abilities start changing odds. Most of this design is wiring existing systems together, not new systems.

The less obvious takeaway concerns *where* exclusivity should live. In this genre the prestige comes from scarcity and history: being at the Meteor Shower, finishing the Star row, grinding the Recycler. It does not come from the price tag. Paid exclusivity is also where every documented backlash and lawsuit lands. Keeping the coveted orbs earned and the sold ones guaranteed and unstealable is the compliant choice for a kids' game, and it also keeps the prestige intact. The open questions can only be answered by data: how much the new abilities shift the econ sim's milestones, and whether players read family carry without a tutorial beat. Run Stage 1 through `econ_sim.py` and one playtest before committing numbers for the later stages.
